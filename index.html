Core product framework

One build, three evidence-based stages

Each pillar meets students at a different point of risk — and all three sit on one reviewed evidence layer, closing into a practice loop that builds reflexes over time.

Stage 01 · Before harm

Breach Drills

Recognise · Decide under pressure

Realistic scam simulations for risk-free decision practice, inside the apps students actually use.

  • SMS, Gmail, Instagram, WhatsApp & social-post scenarios
  • Four reply choices, framed as “what would you really do?”
Stage 02 · In the feed

AI Reality Check

Judge real vs. generated

Interactive “spot the fake” challenges for AI-generated images and fabricated articles.

  • Swipe genuine vs. AI images, with artefact-by-artefact clues
  • Compare two news stories; sources stay hidden until you commit
Stage 03 · After an incident

Digital First Aid

Recover · Know who to contact

Calm, step-by-step response guidance and official reporting pathways for when something has already happened.

  • Five incident paths, from scams to blackmail and leaked passwords
  • Checklists route students to banks, platforms and official help
Evidence foundation — the Case Library & Open-Data pipeline

Every drill traces to a dated, reviewed Australian incident (Scamwatch, ACCC, CommBank, ABC News). No scenario ships without a real source behind it — and AI may only reword an approved base case, never change the safety lesson.

Stage 01 · Breach Drills

Choose a reply before the scammer chooses for you

Each drill opens a faithful replica of a messaging app, clearly labelled a training simulation with no real links. A “Pause before you act” moment presents four honest options — two unsafe, one safe, one bystander — with no obvious “correct answer” styling.

  • Feedback tells the consequence story: Good choice or Worth a second look — never “wrong”.
  • “Try other choices” lets students safely explore every outcome; “What this teaches” names the reflex.
  • AI-personalised variants reword an approved base case to match the student's world — the safety lesson is locked.

Evidence foundation · Case Library

Real incidents, reviewed before release

Nineteen dated Australian cases — pattern descriptions, trend reports and specific incidents — curated from Scamwatch, the ACCC, ASIC, CommBank and ABC News. Students filter by context, search the archive, and follow every case to its original source.

  • Grounded, not generic: each debrief and drill cites a real, clickable source.
  • Cases unlock as practice progresses — a traceable case-file archive.

Stage 02 · AI Reality Check

Train the eye for generated content

A swipe-card challenge asks students to judge six images as genuine or AI-generated — tap to zoom, guess freely, then receive a precise list of artefacts: blurred hands, chaotic hair, impossible reflections.

  • Article mode: compare two news stories and pick the genuine one; bylines and sources stay hidden until the verdict.
  • “Not sure? Have a guess — we'll show you why.” No penalty for looking.

Stage 03 · Digital First Aid

When it's already gone wrong, know exactly what to do

The recovery path opens with “You are not in trouble.” Students pick what happened — scammed, blackmailed, leaked password, fake account, private content shared — and get an ordered, tickable checklist.

  • Urgent-first ordering: contact the bank before anything else when money is involved.
  • Official routes built in — Scamwatch, ReportCyber, IDCARE — plus tell-a-trusted-adult guidance. No free-text disclosure, no judgement.

The loop · My Progress

Progress you can see — and feel

A dashboard turns every attempt into evidence of growth: completion rings for drills and reality checks, a rolling accuracy score, peer percentile encouragement, and an accuracy-over-time chart.

  • Every choice is logged per participant and powers the personalised journey and unlocked cases.
  • Onboarding tours, dark mode and accessible, mobile-tested UI keep the door open for everyone.
